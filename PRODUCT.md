## Platform

web

## Users

Business owners and professionals who need a digital solution but are not technical: small local businesses (clinics, real estate, law offices, aesthetics studios), independent professionals, and companies looking to automate processes. The audience is deliberately mixed; no single segment is prioritized yet. They arrive evaluating whether Juan Pablo can be trusted to build their site, landing page, or automation.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio and conversion site for Juan Pablo (brand JPI), a web developer offering digital solutions. It presents services, shows project work, and turns visitors into conversations. Success means a visitor starts a WhatsApp conversation.

## Positioning

A professional specialized in building modern digital experiences for businesses, turning business needs into digital solutions (confirmed brief, 2026-09-19). Not "a developer who does a bit of everything". One person covers the whole path from first conversation to publication: websites and landing pages, 24h automated customer service, process automations with AI and N8N, and custom solutions. The client talks directly to the person who builds the work.

## Product Principles

1. Every path leads to WhatsApp; make starting a conversation effortless.
2. Honesty over polish: show concept work as concept work, never fabricate proof.
3. Speak to non-technical owners about outcomes (trust, better service, time saved), not tech jargon. Priority: result > experience > solution > technology.
4. Direction over decoration: composition, hierarchy, type and rhythm instead of effects.
5. Mobile-first (inferred, not confirmed): traffic likely arrives via Instagram and WhatsApp links on phones.
