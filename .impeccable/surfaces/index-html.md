# Surface brief: index.html (homepage)

Scope: the whole one-page portfolio. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: non-technical business owners (clinics, real estate, law offices, local services) deciding whether to trust Juan Pablo / JPI with a site or automation, often on a phone.
Action: start a WhatsApp conversation (primary); see projects (secondary).
Proof on hand: three concept sites (Nóbrega Imóveis, Lumière Estética, Andrade & Bittencourt Adv), labeled "Esboço" quietly; real studio portrait. No testimonials, metrics, prices or timelines.
Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS, no libraries; pinned dark navy + electric blue world; honest content only.
Memorable moment: the projects index, names at display scale with the site preview following the cursor, opening into a case sheet.
Unresolved: real testimonials (none yet); prices/timelines (not stated).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a studio index, not a template stack. Work, services and process are read as ruled typographic rows at display scale; images surface when you reach a name. Refuses the icon-card grid, the glow-on-everything dev portfolio and the kicker-above-every-heading rhythm.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black navy void (#03050d) continuous with the portrait's own background, hairline rules in blue-gray, white type in Mona Sans (expanded widths for display, normal for text), electric blue (#2f5bff) rationed to the primary action, active states and one drenched closing field. Pill actions, square-cornered imagery, no glows, no gradient text.

STORY: In one viewport the owner learns who (Juan, portrait + caption), what (sites, landing pages, 24h WhatsApp attendance), for whom (businesses) and the difference (you talk directly to the person who builds it). Projects prove craft, solutions translate into business problems, process and commitments remove risk, the blue close asks for the message.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 12-col grid. Left 7 cols: H1 "Sites à altura do negócio que você construiu." at ~6rem, subline under it, primary pill "Conversar no WhatsApp" + text link "Ver projetos". Right 5 cols bleeding off the right and top edges: the portrait, feathered into the void, crossed by a thin electric "/" (the stroke of the </> mark). Caption row under the portrait: name, city, availability dot. Header: </> Juan Pablo, five links, WhatsApp pill.

FORM: Índice de estúdio, candidate 7 of my ordered list of 7, seed key a880b477. Signature interaction: cursor-following preview on project rows (focus-anchored for keyboard, thumbnails on touch), opening a case sheet dialog (Problema, Direção, Solução, Resultado visual).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
